## ✨ Features

- **Switch outcomes without starting a new Codex task.** An explicit Task
  Bridge reassignment archives the previous membership, attaches the same chat
  to the exact target outcome, and keeps the full history visible.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Correct outcome time after a deliberate task switch.** Only the unfinished
  turn and future work follow the new outcome; every completed turn remains on
  its original Task Board card.
- **No silent attribution on conflicting exact IDs.** Task Bridge pauses
  outcome timing and asks for an explicit switch or confirmation instead of
  continuing to charge the stale membership.
